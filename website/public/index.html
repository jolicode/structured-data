Every syntax

JSON-LD blocks, Microdata and RDFa attributes are read from a whole page, or a lone JSON-LD document. 100% compliant with the JSON-LD 1.1 specification: every W3C test that applies passes. Run its algorithms in the playground.

schema.org and Google

Types and properties are checked against schema.org, and against what the Google Rich Results require.

Private by design

The jolicode/structured-data PHP library runs in this page, compiled to WebAssembly: the document you paste never leaves your browser. Only the URL of a page you load is sent to our server.
